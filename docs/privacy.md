# Privacy Policy

Effective September 8, 2026

This policy covers meldh.com and messages sent to Meldh LLC through the contact addresses published on this site. Olune has a separate [Privacy Policy](https://olune.ai/privacy/) describing the app, its on-device data, and reports submitted from the app.

## The Website

Meldh.com has no account system, advertising, analytics, tracking pixels, or marketing cookies operated by Meldh. The site is hosted through GitHub Pages. GitHub may process ordinary request information, including IP addresses and browser details, to deliver and secure the site under [GitHub's Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Messages You Send

If you email Meldh, we receive your email address and the contents of your message. Meldh uses that information to answer you, investigate the matter you describe, maintain necessary business records, protect its products and users, and meet legal obligations. Meldh email is hosted through Google Workspace, so Google processes and stores that correspondence under [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Messages sent through Olune's in-app report function follow the narrower report rules in the [Olune Privacy Policy](https://olune.ai/privacy/#reports-you-choose-to-make). Meldh does not use support correspondence or Olune reports for advertising or model training.

## Retention and Deletion

Meldh keeps ordinary correspondence only as long as reasonably needed for the purpose of the exchange, necessary business records, disputes, or legal obligations. The Olune policy gives the specific retention practice for response reports. You may request deletion of information you sent by emailing [support@meldh.com](mailto:support@meldh.com). Include the Olune report ID when the request concerns a response report. Legal obligations or claims may require limited records to be preserved.

## Links to Other Services

Links on this site lead to independent services. Those services receive ordinary network information associated with your visit and apply their own terms and privacy policies.

## Children

Meldh.com is a company and research website for a general audience. Meldh does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent personal information to Meldh, contact [support@meldh.com](mailto:support@meldh.com).

## Changes and Contact

Material changes will appear on this page with a revised effective date. Privacy questions and requests may be sent to [support@meldh.com](mailto:support@meldh.com).
